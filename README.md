## The 4-Step Non-Dilutive Approval Playbook (Unlocking $500k+ with $0 VC Funding)

1. **Corporate Domain & Cryptographic Email Hygiene (`SPF` + `2048-bit DKIM` + `DMARC`):**
   Cloud and GPU grant reviewers automatically reject `@gmail.com` / `@outlook.com` addresses or domains without a live product page. Deploy your product landing page over HTTPS and configure `SPF`, `2048-bit RSA DKIM`, and `DMARC` on your primary corporate domain before applying.
2. **Anchor Partner Multipliers (`NVIDIA Inception` + `Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub`):**
   Both **NVIDIA Inception** and **Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub** accept bootstrapped AI software companies with `$0` institutional funding. Once approved in NVIDIA Inception, use your membership status as partner verification to unlock higher tiers in **Google for Startups Cloud Program** (up to `$350,000` AI Scale) and **AWS Activate Portfolio** (`$25,000–$100,000`).
3. **Cloud Organization & Billing Unification:**
   For Google Cloud and AWS, ensure your corporate domain is verified inside **Google Workspace / Cloud Identity** (`Organization ID` + active `Billing Account`) before submitting your startup application so credits deposit without manual identity challenges.
4. **Multi-Cloud Inference & Compute Mesh:**
   Combine **Google Vertex AI** (Gemini + Claude Partner Models), **Azure OpenAI**, **AWS Bedrock**, and **Cloudflare Workers AI** so your engineering team and autonomous IDE agents operate with zero single-provider downtime.
